For the step potential

$$ V(x)=
\begin{cases}
0, & x<0 \\
V, & x\geq0
\end{cases}
$$

define the wave numbers

$$
k_1=\frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar},
\qquad
k_2=\frac{\sqrt{2m(E-V+i0)}}{\hbar}.
$$

The same expressions can be used regardless of whether $E>V$ or $E<V$:

$$
r=\frac{k_1-k_2}{k_1+k_2},
\qquad
t=\frac{2k_1}{k_1+k_2}.
$$

The wavefunction is

$$
\psi(x)=
\begin{cases}
e^{ik_1x}+r e^{-ik_1x}, & x<0 \\
t e^{ik_2x}, & x\geq0
\end{cases}
$$

Using complex numbers for $k_2$ allows the same calculation to handle both cases.
