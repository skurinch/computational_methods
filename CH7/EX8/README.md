The normalization constants depend on the orbital overlap $S$:

$$
S=e^{-d}\left(1+d+\frac{d^2}{3}\right),
$$

$$
N_s=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2(1+S)}},
\qquad
N_a=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2(1-S)}}.
$$

The total energy includes both the Hamiltonian and the electrostatic repulsion between the protons:

$$
E_{\mathrm{tot}}(d)=
H+\frac{1}{d}.
$$

Since $E_s(d)$ dips below the dissociation limit of $−0.5$ Hartree and approaches that limit as $d \to \infty$, the energy curve has a minimum below the energy of a separated hydrogen atom and proton. This minimum corresponds to an equilibrium bond length, and the system is bound.
